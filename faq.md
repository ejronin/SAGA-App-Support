# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is SAGA?

SAGA — Shawn's Assembly Guide Authoring — is an Autodesk Fusion add-in for building illustrated assembly guides from the Fusion model and view already being used to design the product.

## Who is SAGA for?

SAGA is intended for makers, independent product designers, 3D-printable product creators, small hardware sellers, and similar users who need clear assembly documentation without an enterprise documentation system.

## Does SAGA upload my Fusion model or project files?

No. SAGA projects, captures, instructions, BOM information, and generated PDFs stay in the local folders chosen by the user. SAGA may contact Autodesk only to verify Marketplace access.

## Is SAGA a subscription?

No. SAGA is sold as a one-time purchase.

## Can I try SAGA before buying it?

Yes. The paid Marketplace listing includes Autodesk's optional 30-day trial. The trial is fully functional and does not watermark PDFs or disable customer branding.

Autodesk controls the trial and purchase entitlement. SAGA does not run a separate local trial clock. When Autodesk reports that access is no longer valid, continued authoring and publication require a purchase. Existing projects and previously generated PDFs are not deleted or altered.

## Can I use SAGA for commercial work?

Yes. A valid SAGA licence allows users to create manuals and other output for their own products, client work, and commercial sales. Users keep ownership of their own models, projects, instructions, images, branding, and published output.

## Does SAGA support macOS?

No. SAGA 1.0 supports Windows only.

## Does SAGA start automatically with Fusion?

No. Automatic startup is disabled by default. Start SAGA from **Utilities > Scripts and Add-Ins**. Fusion may allow users to enable automatic startup later.

## Is the SAGA source code available in this repository?

No. This repository contains public support and policy documents only. SAGA is proprietary software.

## Does SAGA replace Fusion Drawings or Fusion Animation?

No. SAGA focuses on illustrated assembly-guide authoring and publication. It is not intended to replace Fusion Drawings, Fusion Animation, or an enterprise PLM system.

## How do I get support?

Email **sagaappsupport@gmail.com**. Include the SAGA version, Fusion version, Windows version, exact error message, and steps that reproduce the problem.
