# SAGA Refund Policy

**Effective date: August 5, 2026**

SAGA is sold as a one-time software purchase through the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace.

A fully functional evaluation period is provided so users can confirm that SAGA works with their Fusion setup and fits their workflow before buying it. Because of that evaluation period, completed purchases are generally final.

A refund may still be considered when:

- The same purchase was charged more than once
- A valid purchase cannot be activated and the problem cannot be resolved through reasonable support
- A refund is required by applicable law
- Autodesk, PayPal, or another payment processor requires or authorizes the refund under its rules

Refunds are not normally provided because a user changed their mind, no longer needs the software, expected an undocumented feature, or wants SAGA to perform work outside its published scope.

To request a refund, email **sagaappsupport@gmail.com** with the purchaser's name, purchase email, order or transaction reference, purchase date, and a clear explanation of the request. Do not send payment passwords, full card numbers, or other sensitive financial information.

Refund decisions may depend on Autodesk Marketplace and payment-processor records. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be waived.
