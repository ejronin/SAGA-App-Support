**Effective date: August 5, 2026**

SAGA is submitted to the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace as a paid, one-time-purchase application. It is not a subscription.

Under the current SAGA 1.0 licensing model, a purchase includes the version purchased and any 1.x maintenance updates that Shawn Gordon chooses to release. Maintenance updates may include defect fixes, compatibility corrections, security fixes, and small workflow improvements.

A purchase does not promise:

- Lifetime updates
- Support for every future Fusion release
- New features on a fixed schedule
- Free access to a future major version
- Continued support for obsolete SAGA or Fusion versions

## Fusion compatibility

Fusion changes over time. The publisher may release compatibility corrections when practical, but a future Autodesk change may require investigation, a workaround, a later SAGA update, or a change in the supported Fusion versions. Purchasing SAGA does not guarantee compatibility with every future Fusion release.

## Major versions

A future major release, such as SAGA 2.0, may be offered separately as a paid product or upgrade. Any price, eligibility, or discount would be announced before that release; the SAGA 1.0 purchase does not create a right to a future major version.

## Feature requests

Feature requests may be reviewed for future development, but they do not create a commitment, delivery date, or entitlement to implementation.

## Ending support for an older version

When support for an older version ends, notice will be posted in the [Release Notes](release-notes.md) or Marketplace listing when practical. Ending support does not delete the installed software or the user's local project files.

See the [Support Policy](support.md) for current support scope and version-support terms.
